# Roles and their jobs
keywords: role, top, jungle, mid, adc, bot, support, lane, position, rol, koridor, ormancı, orta, nişancı, destek, görev, ne yapmalı

Draft notes (to be reviewed by a high-elo player).

## Top
- Often an island lane: win your lane or hold it without dying, use Teleport to join fights or flank.
- Later: side-lane pressure (split push) or frontline / engage in team fights, depending on the champion.

## Jungle
- Clear camps, gank lanes with good chances, control objectives, track the enemy jungler.
- Pathing: start on the side where you want to be when lanes push; gank lanes that are pushed toward you or have crowd control.

## Mid
- Central lane with short paths to both sides: push the wave, then roam or help the jungler at the river.
- Often the main damage or playmaker of the team.

## ADC (bot)
- Main sustained damage later in the game; needs farm and items.
- In fights: stay alive, hit whatever is in range safely, do not walk forward first.

## Support
- Lane: protect or enable the ADC, zone the enemy, control vision.
- Later: roam, ward and sweep, start or peel fights depending on the champion (engage, enchanter, mage).

## Everyone
- Know your champion's job in fights: engage, peel, damage, flank or split.
