# Macro: mid and late game
keywords: macro, mid game, late game, split push, side lane, rotation, grouping, map control, makro, orta oyun, geç oyun, split, yan koridor, rotasyon, toplanmak, harita kontrolü, ne yapmalıyım, oyunu nasıl kazanırım

Draft notes (to be reviewed by a high-elo player).

## After laning
- Lanes break up after the first towers. Move as a group around the next objective, with waves pushed into side lanes first.
- The 1-3-1 or 1-4 setup: one strong duelist pushes a side lane while the rest hold or pressure elsewhere.

## Side lanes
- Push side waves when it is safe (you know where the enemies are). Do not sit in a side lane deep with no vision when an objective is coming.
- Catching side waves is gold and tempo; let the champion who can survive alone do it.

## Closing games
- With a lead: take vision, siege towers with waves, force the enemy to answer. Do not chase into fog.
- With Baron or Elder: push with waves together, take inhibitors, then end or reset.

## When behind
- Farm safely, defend towers with waves, wait for enemy mistakes and scaling. Look for picks with vision rather than 5v5 fights.
