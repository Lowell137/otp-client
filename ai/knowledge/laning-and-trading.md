# Laning and trading
keywords: laning, lane phase, trade, trading, harass, all in, poke, cooldown, level 2, level 3, level 6, power spike, koridor, takas, trade atmak, hasar alışverişi, seviye, güç anı, all-in, poke

Draft notes (to be reviewed by a high-elo player).

## Trading
- Trade when the enemy uses an important ability, goes for a last-hit, or is on cooldown.
- Short trades: hit with your strongest combo and step back before they answer.
- Fight near your minions: the side with more minions takes extra damage from them.

## Power spikes
- Level 2 first: the side that hits level 2 first (usually the one that clears the first wave plus part of the second) can often win a fight.
- Level 3, level 6 (ultimate), and finished items are common spikes. Know yours and the enemy's.
- Do not fight into an enemy right after they complete an important item or level up their ultimate when you have not.

## Matchups
- Learn who wins early and who scales. Play safe in losing matchups, ask for help, and farm.
- The assistant's counters tool gives matchup win rates; the Build page has matchup builds.

## Summoner spells
- If the enemy used Flash, that is a window to play aggressive (the overlay's spell timers help).

## Staying alive
- Dying early gives gold and pressure to the enemy. When unsure, farm safe and wait for help or a better window.
