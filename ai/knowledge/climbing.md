# Climbing and improving
keywords: climb, rank up, elo, improve, one trick, champion pool, review, replay, tilt, mental, mute, yükselmek, rank atlamak, gelişmek, tek şampiyon, otp, şampiyon havuzu, tekrar izlemek, tilt, mental

Draft notes (to be reviewed by a high-elo player).

## Champion pool
- A small pool (one or two champions per role, or one-tricking) lets you focus on decisions instead of mechanics. The app is built around one-tricks for this reason.
- Pick champions that are fine into most matchups and that you enjoy.

## Reviewing games
- After a loss, look at deaths: why did it happen, what information did you have? The assistant's last_game review shows items, damage and who was fed.
- Track one habit at a time (CS per minute, deaths before 15 minutes, wards).

## Mental
- Mute when chat becomes a distraction. A loss streak is a good time to stop or play a normal game.
- Focus on what you control: your farm, your deaths, your map awareness.
