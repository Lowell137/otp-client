# Vision and warding
keywords: vision, ward, control ward, pink, sweep, trinket, oracle, görüş, ward atmak, totem, kontrol totemi, pembe, temizlemek, harita

Draft notes (to be reviewed by a high-elo player).

## Basics
- Vision wins fights before they start: you see who is missing and where they come from.
- Wards are most useful on the paths the enemy uses to reach you or an objective, not deep in random places.

## When and where
- Laning: ward the side your enemy jungler is likely on (river bush, tri-bush, the entry near your lane) around when they might gank.
- Before objectives: ward and clear around the pit a minute ahead; it is easier than fighting blind.
- When ahead: deeper wards in the enemy jungle to find the jungler and pick targets.
- When behind: ward your own jungle entrances and play safe.

## Control wards and sweeping
- Buy control wards often; they deny enemy vision in a key bush or pit area.
- Supports and junglers usually switch to the sweeper to clear enemy vision before fights.

## Reading the map
- Look at the map every few seconds. Missing enemies are a threat; three missing usually means a play somewhere.
