# Wave management
keywords: wave, minion, freeze, slow push, fast push, crash, bounce, reset, dalga, minyon, dalga yönetimi, dondurma, freeze atmak, itmek, push, çarptırmak, koridor

Draft notes (to be reviewed by a high-elo player). Concepts, not patch numbers.

## Why it matters
- Minions are gold and experience. Where the wave sits decides who is safe, who can roam and who can be ganked.
- A wave near your tower is safe for you and dangerous for the enemy laner (long walk, exposed to your jungler).

## Freeze
- Keep the wave just outside your tower by last-hitting only and letting the enemy wave stay slightly bigger (about 3 more enemy caster minions in front of yours keeps it still).
- Use it when you are ahead (deny gold and XP) or when you are weak and want safety.
- Breaks when the enemy wave gets too big, when you auto too much, or when your own wave grows.

## Slow push
- Last-hit only, let your wave grow over two or three waves. Ends in a big crash under the enemy tower.
- Use before a recall, a roam, a dragon / grubs fight, or to dive.

## Fast push and crash
- Kill the wave quickly so it reaches the enemy tower; the enemy loses minions under tower and you get free time.
- Do it before recalling (so you do not lose a wave), before objectives, or when your jungler is near.

## Bounce
- A wave that crashes into a tower comes back to your side by itself. Crash, recall, and the wave tends to return toward you.

## Common mistakes
- Pushing with no vision and no idea where the enemy jungler is.
- Recalling with a wave about to crash into your tower (you lose it).
- Hitting the wave aimlessly instead of choosing freeze, slow push or crash.
