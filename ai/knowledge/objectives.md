## Priority
- Objectives are won by setting up first: lanes pushed, vision around the pit, health and items up.
- A free objective is one the enemy cannot contest (they are dead, far away, or their lanes are under tower). Take those.
- Trading objectives on opposite sides of the map is fine when you cannot contest.

## Dragons
- Each dragon gives a team-wide bonus; stacking toward the Dragon Soul is strong in long games.
- Elder Dragon late in the game is often decisive.

## Voidgrubs and Rift Herald (top side)
- Voidgrubs help destroy towers; Rift Herald is used to break a tower and open the map.
- Strong for teams with an early side-lane or tower-pushing plan.

## Baron Nashor
- The Baron buff makes minion waves much stronger; use it to take towers and inhibitors, not to wander.
- Risky without vision; teams often lose games by starting Baron with enemies alive nearby.

## Towers
- First towers open the map and give gold. Turret plating (early game) rewards pressure in lane.
