# Team fights
keywords: teamfight, team fight, fight, engage, peel, frontline, backline, positioning, focus, takım savaşı, savaş, engage, peel, ön saf, arka saf, pozisyon, odak, kimi vurmalı

Draft notes (to be reviewed by a high-elo player).

## Before the fight
- Fight with numbers, vision and summoner spells up. Do not take even fights when the enemy has their ultimates and you do not.
- Know your team's plan: dive the backline, or front-to-back (hit what is in front, protect your carries).

## During
- Frontline / engage: start the fight on a good target or soak damage; do not run in alone.
- Carries: hit the closest safe target; stay in range of your team.
- Peel: keep enemy divers off your carries.
- Focus: the target you can kill safely is usually better than the one you want to kill.

## After
- Turn a won fight into an objective (tower, dragon, Baron) instead of chasing.
- After a lost fight: back off, keep waves away from your base, do not give more kills.

## Common mistakes
- Fighting without the jungler or with a member far away in a side lane.
- Face-checking bushes before a fight.
