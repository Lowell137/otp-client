# Recall timing and tempo
keywords: recall, back, base, reset, gold, tempo, b atmak, base'e dönmek, geri dönmek, ne zaman b, altın, alışveriş, zamanlama

Draft notes (to be reviewed by a high-elo player).

## When to recall
- After crashing a wave (you lose the least), with enough gold for a meaningful item component.
- When low on health or mana and staying risks a death that costs more than the lost minions.
- Before an objective spawns, so you come back full with items (plan the timing ahead).

## When not to
- With a big enemy wave about to hit your tower.
- Right before an objective your team is about to fight for, unless you can be back in time.

## Tempo
- Tempo is who moves first. The side that recalls at the right time, buys, and arrives first at the next fight usually wins it.
- Count rough enemy timers: if the enemy laner just recalled, you have a window to push, roam or help the jungler.

## Spending gold
- Plan the next component before recalling (the overlay's Build window shows the next item and gold short).
- Control wards and potions are cheap and often worth more than a slightly bigger component.
