## Core and situational
- The core build (the first two or three items most players buy) gives the champion its main power.
- After the core, react to the enemy: armor against mostly physical damage, magic resist against magic, anti-heal against healing.

## Defensive stats
- Armor reduces physical damage; magic resist reduces magic damage. Health is good against both but weaker against percent-health damage.
- Buy defense against the enemy that is actually dangerous (the fed one), not just the team average.

## Anti-heal (Grievous Wounds)
- Needed against champions or teams that heal or drain a lot. One or two players buying it is usually enough.

## Penetration
- Lethality and armor penetration help against armor; magic penetration against magic resist. Very good when the enemy stacks one resist.

## Boots
- Armor boots against auto-attackers and physical damage; magic resist / tenacity boots against magic damage and crowd control.
