# Jungle basics
keywords: jungle, jungler, clear, path, pathing, gank, counter jungle, invade, smite, camps, orman, ormancı, gank atmak, rota, yol, invade, çalmak, kamp, smite

Draft notes (to be reviewed by a high-elo player).

## Clearing and pathing
- Plan where you want to be when the first waves push: start on the opposite side and clear toward the lane you want to play around.
- Full clear for farm-heavy champions; a faster path to an early gank for gank-heavy champions.

## Ganking
- Best targets: a lane pushed toward your side, an enemy with no escape (Flash down), allies with crowd control.
- Avoid ganking a lane where the enemy is under their tower with full health and your laner has no follow-up.

## Tracking the enemy jungler
- From their start and clear speed you can guess where they are. Warn your lanes, or go to the other side to take camps or objectives.

## Counter-jungling
- Take enemy camps when you know the enemy jungler is on the other side and your lanes have priority (pushed).

## Objectives
- Junglers usually call objectives; time recalls and clears so you are near the pit when it spawns.
